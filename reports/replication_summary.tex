\section{Project Overview}
This project reproduces the Treasury spot-futures spread construction used in the appendix of \textit{Segmented Arbitrage}
by Emil Siriwardane, Adi Sunderam, and Jonathan Wallen (2023) \href{https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/24-030_1506d32b-3190-4144-8c75-a2326b87f81e.pdf}{(link)}.
In this repository, spreads are computed as implied repo minus interpolated OIS (in basis points) for five tenor labels
(\texttt{2Y}, \texttt{5Y}, \texttt{10Y}, \texttt{20Y}, \texttt{30Y}) using Bloomberg futures/OIS inputs and CRSP/WRDS bond inputs.

The implementation in \texttt{src/calc\_spread.py} computes implied repo directly from futures and CTD-bond fields, evaluates both first- and last-delivery windows inferred from contract month, and retains the maximum implied repo for each date-tenor pair before subtracting interpolated OIS. In the current staged outputs, the arbitrage spread series runs from \texttt{2010-01-04} through \texttt{2026-01-30}.

All core deliverables are scripted through \texttt{doit}: staged inputs, spread outputs, static and interactive charts, supporting tables/figures, and this report.

\section{Data Sources}
\noindent The replication is built from two staged raw inputs: \texttt{bloomberg.parquet} and
\texttt{TFZ\_IRR.parquet}. In the automated pipeline (\texttt{dodo.py}), these files are copied from
\texttt{data\_manual/} into \texttt{\_data/} and then used by \texttt{src/calc\_spread.py}. The live pull scripts
(\texttt{src/pull\_bloomberg.py} and \texttt{src/pull\_CRSP.py}) are configured for
\texttt{2010-01-01} to \texttt{2026-02-28}; the staged data and outputs used in this writeup include observations
through early 2026 (with spread outputs through \texttt{2026-01-30}).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Bloomberg futures and OIS inputs (via \texttt{xbbg}):}
for second-deferred Treasury futures (\texttt{TU2}, \texttt{FV2}, \texttt{TY2}, \texttt{WN2}, \texttt{US2}),
the code pulls \texttt{PX\_LAST}, \texttt{PX\_VOLUME}, \texttt{FUT\_CTD\_CUSIP},
\texttt{FUT\_CNVS\_FACTOR}, and \texttt{CURRENT\_CONTRACT\_MONTH\_YR}. It also pulls OIS
\texttt{PX\_LAST} for 2M/3M/4M/5M/6M/9M tenors (\texttt{USSOB}, \texttt{USSOC}, \texttt{USSOD},
\texttt{USSOE}, \texttt{USSOF}, \texttt{USSOI}).

\item \textbf{How Bloomberg fields are used:}
\texttt{PX\_LAST} and \texttt{FUT\_CNVS\_FACTOR} form the futures leg in implied repo;
\texttt{FUT\_CTD\_CUSIP} is the key used to merge to CRSP bond records by date and CUSIP;
\texttt{CURRENT\_CONTRACT\_MONTH\_YR} is parsed to infer first and last delivery business days
(used to evaluate both delivery choices). OIS points are linearly interpolated to the tenor-specific
holding period and converted to bps for the financing benchmark in the spread.

\item \textbf{CRSP/WRDS bond inputs:}
\texttt{src/pull\_CRSP.py} queries \texttt{crspm.tfz\_dly} joined to \texttt{crspm.tfz\_iss}
for Treasury note/bond issues (\texttt{itype} in 1,2), retrieving bid/ask, accrued interest,
coupon rate, issue/maturity dates, and CUSIP. The script constructs
\texttt{clean\_price} (mid bid/ask), keeps beginning accrued interest, and carries coupon metadata.

\item \textbf{How CRSP fields are used:}
clean price and coupon schedule inputs produce the bond-side terms in the implied repo formula
(\(A_e\), \(A_b\), \(I_c\), \(d_1\), \(d_2\)). For each tenor-date, the code computes implied repo for
first and last delivery windows and keeps the maximum, then computes
Spread \(=\) Implied Repo \(-\) Interpolated OIS (in bps).

\item \textbf{Key constraints and assumptions in this repo:}
Bloomberg historical delivery-date fields are not relied on; delivery bounds are inferred from contract month.
CRSP does not provide an explicit coupon schedule in the pulled tables, so the code assumes semiannual coupons
(\texttt{coupon\_frequency}=2) and \texttt{ACT/ACT} basis and derives previous/next coupon dates from maturity.
\end{itemize}

\section{Replication Assessment}
\subsection{Where the replication succeeded}
The replication succeeded in producing a complete, automated spread pipeline from staged Bloomberg and CRSP inputs through implied repo, interpolated OIS, and final tenor-level spreads. In the current outputs, all five tenor series are populated and the arbitrage spread panel spans \texttt{2010-01-04} to \texttt{2026-01-30}, with supporting tables and figures generated from the same inputs.

\subsection{Challenges and limitations}
The hardest parts were data and mapping frictions: Bloomberg/WRDS access constraints, aligning daily futures records to CTD bond CUSIPs, and handling incomplete historical delivery-date fields. On the bond side, CRSP does not directly provide all coupon schedule fields needed for carry calculations, so we use transparent assumptions (semiannual coupons and ACT/ACT conventions) that may still leave small differences versus the original implementation.
